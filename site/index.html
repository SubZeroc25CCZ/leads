What exactly is in a lead?

Thirteen CSV fields: lead reference, owner name, property address, city, state, ZIP, county, signals, case opened, case age, mailing state, first seen and an outcome link. Offer attribution is appended when a buyer has configured it.

Where does the data come from?

Public Florida city and county code-enforcement records, matched to Property Appraiser tax rolls where owner and mailing data are available. We do not scrape HTML search UIs.

How does the free sample work?

Tell the concierge your county, volume and work email. It checks the live shelf, writes a chat-sourced request and sends a 10-record CSV sample to your inbox.

What is the replacement policy?

If a record is wrong or materially stale, use its outcome link and tell us what failed. We would rather retire a bad row than resell it.

How does a team pilot work?

Teams can request a county and volume pilot with CSV, portal or webhook delivery. Pilot terms are a conversation; enterprise pricing is not published here.
