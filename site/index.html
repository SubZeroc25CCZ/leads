The pipeline

From county filing to closable lead

No recycled lists. Every row starts as a government record and carries the date we pulled it.

Step 01 · Daily

Harvest

Automated pulls from county and city open-data and code-enforcement systems each morning. New sources are added as their portals are onboarded.

Step 02 · Same run

Match & score

Each parcel is matched to the Property Appraiser record for owner name, mailing address and ZIP. Absentee status is derived by comparing mailing address to the property address. Signals stack into a score.

Step 03 · On order

Delivered

CSV filtered to your counties, ZIPs and minimum score. Shared leads go to a maximum of three buyers. Exclusive rows are flagged and never resold.
