THE SIGNAL

A public filing can show a financing event.

When an MCA lender files a UCC-1, the public record names the debtor and secured party. We use that record as a research signal, then apply lender classification, filing-level deduplication, entity checks and registry enrichment.

We do not claim revenue, credit score, requested amount or intent. The buyer decides what to do with the data.

01

Managed lender lists

Known MCA names are maintained explicitly. Generic words are not allowed to decide what enters a client file.

02

Deduped by filing

Aliases are retained in an AKA field; repeated filings are reduced to the newest merchant record with a filing count.

03

Auditable delivery

Every release carries source dates, a QA report, a review queue and a ten-row owner audit.
