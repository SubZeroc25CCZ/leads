Data terms.

The MCA product is public-record trigger data for business-finance research. It is not a form-fill lead service.

What the data is

Records are assembled from public Colorado datasets and may be filtered, deduplicated and enriched from public business-registry sources. A filing is not proof of current intent, need, eligibility or willingness to engage.

We do not provide credit scores, revenue, requested amounts, or claims about financing intent unless they exist in the cited public source. Unknown lender names remain in review.

Use and compliance

The product is supplied for business research and data analysis. Buyers are responsible for their own lawful use, outreach practices, consent requirements, suppression lists and TCPA compliance. LeadMachine does not contact the businesses identified in the data.

Every client-bound file should be reviewed by the owner before use. A sample or row count is not a guarantee of accuracy, conversion, exclusivity or commercial outcome.
